About the yearbook

A place to keep the campus

Every campus has a story. Every memory deserves a place.

Years that move too fast

College Diaries is a quiet place to preserve college memories, campus events, friendships, and the meaningful moments that never quite fit in a timetable. It is a digital yearbook, not a race for attention.

Bring the nervous first walk through the gate, the fest that ran past midnight, the classroom where a hard idea finally clicked, the dust of sports day, and the farewell that made four years feel both long and sudden.

Friendships belong here too. The people who shared notes, umbrellas, canteen tables, and the long way home are part of the story of a batch. This shelf is where those pages can stay.

Accounts and the shelf

Simple, with a real backend

The yearbook is public to read. Sharing a page needs an account, so a memory can carry the student who wrote it. Sign in with the demo student, or create your own account with a name, email, and a password of at least eight characters.

The included server.js is a small Node.js backend with no extra packages. Start it with npm start and open http://localhost:3000. It serves these pages, keeps accounts and memories in data/db.json, hashes passwords with scrypt, and gives each sign-in a seven-day bearer session.

If the backend is not running, the same screens switch to a clearly labelled browser preview. Accounts and added pages are kept in localStorage so the flow can still be tried. Browsing the yearbook needs neither mode.

This is a learning-friendly MVP, not a production auth service. Likes, comments, messages, payments, and password resets are still out of scope. Sample stories always live with the site.
